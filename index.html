My 5-Step Design Process

How I transform ambiguous enterprise requirements into scalable, developer-friendly digital products.

01

Stakeholder interviews, user research, workflow audits, and analyzing user pain points across legacy software.

02

Define

03

Iterative low-fidelity sketches progressing into interactive high-fidelity Figma components and prototypes.

04

Validate

05

Handoff
